About pressure drop in air and gas lines

What pressure drop is acceptable?

For blower and booster piping, keep the total drop small compared with the machine’s pressure rise, often under 5 to 10 %. For vacuum lines, aim for less than a few percent of the absolute pressure, because every millibar lost reduces the vacuum at the point of use.

What velocity should I design for?

Typical targets are 10 to 25 m/s for blower and compressor lines and lower, around 10 to 15 m/s, for vacuum lines. Higher velocity raises noise and pressure loss quickly.

Why does the pipe diameter matter so much?

For the same flow, pressure loss varies roughly with the fifth power of the diameter. Going from 3 in. to 4 in. pipe cuts the straight-pipe loss to roughly a quarter.

Does the calculator work for vacuum?

Yes. Enter the absolute pressure of the line, for example 500 mbar absolute, and the flow at those conditions. The gas density is low in vacuum, so the velocity is high for the same mass flow.
