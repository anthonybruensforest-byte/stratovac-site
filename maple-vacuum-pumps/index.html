Our advantage: the oil heater

Water in the oil is what kills maple pumps

During the season the pump is often stopped, and water stays in the oil. The mix can corrupt the oil and, in time, damage the pump.

Our thermostat-controlled oil heater keeps the oil warm enough for the water to evaporate. The oil stays in good condition and the pump keeps a better vacuum, season after season.

Available on the STROV-0060 to 1200 models. An oil level switch is required for safety.

Questions from maple producers

How many taps can a vacuum pump hold?

It depends on the pumping speed and on how tight your tubing is. As a guide, we count about 37 taps per m³/h of pumping speed at 60 Hz, in line with the capacities CDL publishes for pumps of similar size. A STROV-0060 (36 m³/h) is good for about 1,300 taps, a STROV-0200 (120 m³/h) for about 4,400, and a STROV-0500 (300 m³/h) for about 11,100. Leaks, long lines and a higher vacuum target lower these numbers, so send us your layout and we size it with you.

Why does a maple vacuum pump need an oil heater?

During the season the pump is often stopped and water stays in the oil. The mix corrupts the oil and, in time, damages the pump. A thermostat-controlled oil heater keeps the oil warm enough for the water to evaporate, so the oil stays in good condition and the pump holds a better vacuum, season after season. The heater is available on the STROV-0060 to 1200 models, with an oil level switch required for safety.
