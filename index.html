Fewer parts to leak, wear or fail

Most gas problems come from shaft seals, oil, gears and corrosion. Stratovac machines are designed to take each of them out of the gas path.

How the magnetic drive seals the gas

Torque crosses a solid wall through magnets, so the motor shaft never enters the gas chamber.

  1. 1The motor turns the outer magnets, outside the gas.
  2. 2Their magnetic field crosses the sealed wall, with no hole for a shaft.
  3. 3The inner magnets turn the shaft and both impellers inside the gas.

  • Compact high speed

  • Hazardous-area motors

    Class I Div. 1/2 and Class II Div. 2 motors let the machine install straight into classified areas.

Where Stratovac machines run

From biogas skids to ventilators and nuclear plants, the common thread is a gas that standard blowers handle badly.

Biogas and renewable natural gas

Blowers and boosters that move wet, flammable, H2S-laden gas through upgrading and conditioning systems, and boost it before pipeline injection.

Natural gas treatment

Gas recirculation and regeneration in dryers and PSA units, oxygen removal systems, and fuel-gas boosting for turbine skids.

Hydrogen and fuel cells

Turbo blowers for cathode air or oxygen supply and boosters for hydrogen recirculation, supplied to fuel cell manufacturers.

Collaborative robotics

Two-stage high-speed boosters small enough to mount on the robot or the gripper instead of a central station.

Medical and respiratory

Boosters cleaned and greased to meet breathable-air ISO limits for very low VOC. Used in a mechanical ventilator program built in Canada during COVID-19.

Dental equipment

Light, high-flow boosters for portable dental vacuum, with a nickel coating against caustic cleaning products.

Nuclear and radioactive gas

Custom boosters that circulate radioactive gas: radiation-resistant greases, depleted uranium shielding and a low-speed design for bearing life. Units delivered several years ago are still in operation.

Aquaculture

Air and oxygen injection for recirculating aquaculture systems and live-holding tanks. Oil-free for the safety of live species, built for 24/7 duty.

Compressed air and nitrogen

Electric boosters raise line pressure at the point of use without drawing compressed air from the network. A nitrogen booster for fiber laser cutting is in development.

Our advantage: the oil heater

Water in the oil is what kills maple pumps

During the season the pump is often stopped, and water stays in the oil. The mix can corrupt the oil and, in time, damage the pump.

Our thermostat-controlled oil heater keeps the oil warm enough for the water to evaporate. The oil stays in good condition and the pump keeps a better vacuum, season after season.

Available on the STROV-0060 to 1200 models. An oil level switch is required for safety.
