Dry-running, contact-free claw compressors for clean compressed air at low pressure. Air-cooled, direct driven and built for continuous duty. Eight models from 68 to 600 m³/h, with pressures up to 2.2 bar (32 psi).

Dry running, nothing to wear

The rotors do not touch each other or the housing, so there is no oil in the compression chamber and no wearing contact. The result is clean, oil-free air and low maintenance.

  • Oil-free and contact-free

    Dry-running design. No oil reaches your air, your process or your product.

  • Low maintenance, no wear

    Contact-free rotors, air cooling and a direct drive keep servicing simple.

  • Continuous duty

    High efficiency, built to run non-stop.

  • CE certified

    The whole range is CE certified (declaration of conformity).

Typical applications. Pneumatic conveying, transport, environmental systems, air sparging, fish farming and central low-pressure air systems.

Questions about claw compressors

What is a claw compressor?

A claw compressor is a dry-running rotary compressor. Two claw-shaped rotors turn without touching each other or the housing, so no oil is needed in the compression chamber and the air stays clean. Stratovac claw compressors are air-cooled and directly driven by a flanged motor.

Is the air really oil-free?

The compression chamber is dry-running and contact-free, so no oil is used in it. Gear box oil is kept separate from the air path.

Which pressures and flows are available?

From 68 to 600 m³/h (40 to 353 cfm) at 60 Hz, and 56 to 500 m³/h at 50 Hz, with continuous pressures from 0.6 to 2.2 bar (9 to 32 psi) depending on the model and motor. See the tables above for every combination.

Can it run continuously?

Yes. The range is designed for continuous duty and high efficiency.

What are claw compressors used for?

Pneumatic conveying, transport, environmental systems, air sparging, fish farming and central low-pressure air systems.

How do I choose the model?

Send us the flow you need, the pressure at the outlet, the duty cycle and your electrical supply (60 or 50 Hz). We confirm the model and the motor.
